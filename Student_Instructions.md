- Three defects have been intentionally planted.
- Finding two can earn substantial partial credit if your evidence and reasoning are strong.
- You may report additional suspected defects. You will not lose points merely because a suspected fourth defect is not planted, provided your reasoning is technically sound.
- Focus on evidence: location, formula/value, why it is wrong, correction, impact, estimated age, and verification.
